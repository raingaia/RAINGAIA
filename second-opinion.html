What's included

Specialist matching

We match your case to a U.S. specialist with relevant, verified experience in your specific diagnosis — not a generalist queue.

Record review

Your existing records and reports are reviewed and organized so the specialist has what's needed to form a genuine opinion, not a surface-level read.

Written or video opinion

A structured written report, or a live video consultation where you can ask questions directly — whichever format fits your situation.

Who this is for

Patients who already have a diagnosis and treatment plan at home and want an independent U.S. clinical perspective before deciding how to proceed. The opinion is yours to use however you choose — including sharing it with your local physician — with no obligation to continue with RAINGAIA HEALTH afterward.

How it's different from a full case review

A second opinion is a standalone, self-contained program — it does not include trial matching, application coordination, or travel support. If, after receiving your opinion, you want to pursue a specific U.S. trial or treatment center, that moves into our standard case review process as a separate next step.
