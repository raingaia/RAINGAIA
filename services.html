What We Do

Coordination services, not medical treatment.

Every service below is administrative and logistical in nature. Clinical decisions are always made by licensed physicians at the receiving institution.

Our matching standard

We do not refer patients to whichever institution is easiest to reach. Every match is the result of verified, condition-specific research:

  • Eligibility cross-check — we confirm a trial or program's inclusion criteria against your specific diagnosis and stage before presenting it as an option, rather than surfacing generic listings.
  • Active status verification — we confirm the trial or program is currently enrolling and the center is actively accepting international cases.
  • Physician & institution credentials — we verify the treating specialist's standing and the institution's relevant accreditation before coordination begins.

If no genuine match exists for your case, we tell you plainly rather than proceeding with a poor fit.

01

Clinical trial & center identification

We research active U.S. trials and specialized centers relevant to your diagnosis, including rare and genetic conditions with limited global treatment options — matched using the standard above, not a generic directory.

02

Medical record review & organization

Your records are reviewed, translated where needed, and organized into the format U.S. institutions require for evaluation.

03

Application preparation & submission

We prepare and submit applications to hospitals and trial sites on your behalf, tracking every step of the process.

04

Appointment & evaluation coordination

We schedule required medical evaluations and consultations, aligning timelines between you and the receiving center.

05

Accommodation & hospital process coordination

Once you're in the U.S., we coordinate accommodation logistics and each step of the hospital's intake process — check-in, appointments, and follow-up scheduling. For medical visa applications, we help organize supporting documentation, but visa legal representation is outside our scope — we recommend independent immigration counsel for that step.

06

Ongoing communication support

A consistent point of contact between you or your family and the U.S. medical team for the duration of your case.

Scope & Boundaries

What RAINGAIA HEALTH is — and what it deliberately is not.

What we do

  • Independent case review and clinical trial / center research
  • Medical record organization and application preparation
  • Appointment, evaluation, and travel coordination
  • Ongoing communication support between you and your U.S. medical team
  • Transparent, upfront service fees — charged to patients, not institutions

What we don't do

  • We are not a pharmacy and do not sell, export, or ship medicines
  • We do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment
  • We do not guarantee acceptance into any trial or treatment program
  • We do not accept referral payments or commissions from hospitals or trial sites
  • We do not act as a licensed broker, agent, or immigration attorney — visa legal representation always sits with independent counsel, not us
