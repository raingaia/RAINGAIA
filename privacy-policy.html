1. Information we collect

When you submit a case through our website, we collect the information you provide directly, including your name, country of residence, email address, the condition or diagnosis you describe, and any message or medical records you choose to share with us.

2. How we use your information

  • To review your case and determine whether relevant U.S. clinical trials or treatment centers may apply
  • To communicate with you about your case, our services, and our fee structure
  • To prepare and submit applications on your behalf, if you choose to proceed
  • To coordinate appointments, evaluations, and travel logistics related to your case

We do not use your information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. When we share information

We share your information only as needed to provide the services you request — for example, with a U.S. hospital or trial site as part of an application you have asked us to submit. We do not share your information with hospitals, trial sites, or any third party in exchange for payment or referral compensation.

4. Health-related information

Some information you share with us, such as your diagnosis or medical history, is sensitive health information. We limit what we request at the initial inquiry stage to what is necessary for case review, and we handle any medical records or documentation you provide afterward with additional care and restricted access. RAINGAIA HEALTH is not a covered entity or business associate under HIPAA, but we apply comparable safeguards to protect health information you share with us.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, including under the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), or Turkey's KVKK. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Data security & retention

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. We retain case information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal and recordkeeping obligations, after which it is securely deleted or anonymized.
