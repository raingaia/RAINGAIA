The Process

Four stages, from first contact to an active care plan.

Every case moves through the same structured process — clear, sequential, and transparent about what happens at each stage.

Stage 1

Case submission & review

You share your diagnosis, medical history, and records. Our clinical research team reviews the case to understand what U.S. options may be relevant.

Stage 2

Trial & center matching

We verify eligibility, active enrollment status, and physician credentials before presenting a match — and explain the realistic options honestly, including when no genuine match exists.

Stage 3

Application & coordination

We prepare and submit your application, organize your medical records, and coordinate appointments and evaluations with the receiving institution.

Stage 4

Accommodation, hospital process & ongoing support

If accepted, we coordinate accommodation and each step of the hospital process in the U.S., and remain the communication link between you and your medical team throughout care. Visa document organization is included; visa legal representation is not — we refer you to independent immigration counsel for that.

What to expect at each stage

Stage 1 — Case submission & review (typically 2–3 business days)

You complete our intake form with your diagnosis, relevant medical history, and any records you can share. A member of our clinical research team reviews the case to form an initial view of what U.S. options may be relevant — this stage is included in the evaluation fee.

Stage 2 — Trial & center matching

We cross-check active clinical trials and specialized treatment centers against your diagnosis and eligibility profile, verify current enrollment status and physician credentials, and present the realistic options along with what each would involve. We do not refer you to a partner institution simply because it is convenient for us.

Stage 3 — Application & coordination

Once you choose to proceed, we organize your medical records into the format the receiving institution requires, prepare the application, and coordinate required evaluations and appointments on your behalf.

Stage 4 — Accommodation, hospital process & ongoing support

If you are accepted, we coordinate accommodation logistics and each stage of the hospital's intake and follow-up process in the U.S., and help organize the documentation your visa application requires. Visa legal representation itself is outside our scope — we recommend independent immigration counsel for that step. We remain your point of contact with the U.S. medical team for as long as you need coordination support.
