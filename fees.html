How We're Paid

A simple, transparent fee structure — paid by patients, not institutions.

RAINGAIA HEALTH does not accept referral fees, commissions, or any form of compensation from hospitals, trial sites, or medical institutions. Our fees are paid exclusively by the patients we serve.

01 — Upfront

Evaluation & application fee

Charged at the start of your case for clinical research, trial/center identification, and initial application preparation. Non-refundable, and does not guarantee acceptance into any program.

02 — Upon acceptance

Facilitation fee

Payable if you are accepted into a trial or treatment program and choose to proceed with full coordination — application follow-through, scheduling, and travel support.

03 — Ongoing (optional)

Coordination support

For patients who want continued communication support with their U.S. medical team throughout an extended course of treatment.

RAINGAIA HEALTH does not accept referral fees, commissions, or any form of compensation from hospitals, trial sites, or medical institutions. Our fees are paid exclusively by the patients we serve.

Why this structure

We charge patients directly rather than accepting compensation from hospitals or trial sites. This means our recommendations are based on what fits your case — not on which institution pays us. Exact fee amounts are confirmed during your initial case review, before any payment is requested.
